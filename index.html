How an order runs

Four steps from your number to your name on the collar.

01

Measure

We take your numbers in person or by video call — chest, shoulder, length, nothing guessed.

02

Cut

Fabric is chosen and cut to your exact frame, not a stock size rounded up or down.

03

Stitch

Hand-finished seams and trim, machine-stitched body, so it holds shape wear after wear.

04

Fit

One fitting before you take it home. Adjustments are free until it sits right.

The workshop

Owerri-made, worn everywhere.

Chuks Fashion is based in Owerri, Imo State. We sew for church programmes, weddings, burial ceremonies, and everyday wear. Every piece is cut and finished by hand, and we choose fabric and trim with you before a needle touches thread.

No appointment needed. Send a message with what you’re looking for and when you need it, and we’ll take it from there.
